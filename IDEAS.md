# Ideas — open backlog

Ideas that are not (yet) planned. This file grows over the life of the project; when an idea is
accepted, it moves into `PLAN.md` with a link to its phase.

Legend: 💡 raw idea · 👍 approved by the user, not scheduled yet · 🧪 to prototype

## Agent collaboration
- 💡 **Room lead**: a "lead" desk that splits a request and delegates to the other desks through the office MCP.
- 💡 **Meeting room**: gather several agents around a table for a shared task (brainstorm, cross review).
- 💡 **File locks** (`office_claim_file`) in "shared folder" mode, to avoid concurrent edits.
- 💡 **Auto wake-up**: an idle desk receiving a message from a colleague gets it injected into its terminal (per-room setting).
- 💡 **Room templates**: pre-configured rooms (e.g. "front + back + tests") with initial prompts per desk.

## Supervision / master room
- 💡 **Chat with Haiku**: ask questions about progress ("where is project X at?").
- 💡 **One-line summary per desk** generated by Haiku for the whiteboard (instead of the raw last prompt).
- 💡 **Free-text question detection**: Haiku classifies a desk's last message ("it is asking you something" vs "it is done").
- 💡 **Text-to-speech** reading of the summary on login.
- 💡 **Per-desk stats**: tokens, estimated cost, duration, git branch, changed files, diff viewer.
- 💡 **Global search** across all transcripts of all sessions.
- 💡 **Day replay**: time-lapse of the office built from the timeline.
- 💡 **Import** existing Claude Code sessions (`claude agents`) into a desk.

## 3D world / ambience
- 💡 **Real weather** behind the windows (rain, sun) through a weather API.
- 💡 **Seasons** following the real date: autumn leaves, snow in winter, blossoms in spring.
- 💡 **More character life** (beyond phase 9): stretching, chatting at the water cooler in pairs, lunch breaks.
- 💡 **Master office customization** (decoration, posters, plants).
- 💡 **More realistic rendering**: PBR materials, lightmaps baked in Blender, post-processing (SSAO, bloom), more detailed characters.
- 💡 **Post-processing on the Max graphics level**: ambient occlusion (N8AO), bloom on lamps and screens.
- 💡 **Meeting room** as a real room type (meeting table + chairs + TV props already exist).
- 💡 **Animated doors**, elevator if the office grows beyond one floor (one floor per project group?).
- 💡 **Easter eggs**: a plant wilting when a desk has been in error for a long time, confetti when a long task finishes.

## Performance
- 💡 **Character LOD**: simplified or frozen characters far from the camera; share materials between characters with the same colors.
- 💡 **Smaller assets**: meshopt compression of the GLBs, code-splitting the 3D bundle (1.8 MB today).
- 💡 **Lighter props**: fewer bevel segments on small repeated parts (a bookshelf is ~5k triangles
  because of its books, a keyboard ~1k because of its keys).
- 💡 **Adaptive quality**: lower the pixel ratio automatically when frames take too long.
- 💡 **Adaptive preview rate**: server-side frame rate per subscriber (focused desk faster, others slower).

## Platform
- 👍 **Two-factor authentication** (TOTP) — requested for later.
- 👍 **More languages** besides FR/EN — the i18n architecture is designed for it.
- 💡 **Mobile view**: simplified 2D (map + inbox + terminal).
- 💡 **Push notifications** (phone) when a desk asks a question.
- 💡 **Multi-user**: several humans connected, visible as avatars, with presence.
- 💡 **Deployment**: Docker image / systemd service for the Linux server, HTTPS through a reverse proxy or Tailscale.
